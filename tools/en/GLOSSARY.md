# English glossary (US base, UK marked)

Decided 5-11-2026 with Eduardo: **American English as the base**. Where US and UK differ, the page says it the first time the term appears on that page, like this: `quarter note (UK: crotchet)`. After that, only the US term.

Rules for every EN page:
- US spelling (color, centered, favorite). No "whilst/amongst".
- Plain English, no idioms. Short sentences.
- Note names: letters (C D E F G A B). Fixed-do names (Do Re Mi) are mentioned once as "the solfège names used in Spanish-speaking countries".
- Accidentals: sharp ♯, flat ♭, natural ♮, double sharp 𝄪, double flat 𝄫.
- Octave numbers in scientific pitch notation (A4 = 440 Hz), same as ES (La4).
- Never translate a proper name of the ES site's features word for word if it sounds odd; use the table below.
