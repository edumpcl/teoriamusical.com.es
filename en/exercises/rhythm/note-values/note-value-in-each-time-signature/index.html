Note Values in Each Time Signature

The value of a note in beats depends on the time signature; the relationship between notes does not change (a quarter note (UK: crotchet) always lasts twice as long as an eighth note (UK: quaver)). A dotted quarter note lasts 1½ beats in 2/4 and exactly one beat in 6/8, because in compound meters the beat is the dotted quarter note. In this exercise you see a measure and a note or a rest, and you say how long it lasts, in beats.

How long each note lasts in each time signature

The first step is to look at the time signature and know which note lasts one beat:

  • In the simple meters with a 4 at the bottom (2/4, 3/4 and 4/4) the beat is the quarter note, which divides into two eighth notes.
  • In the compound meters with an 8 at the bottom (6/8, 9/8 and 12/8) the beat is the dotted quarter note, which divides into three eighth notes. That is why each eighth note lasts one third of a beat.
NoteIn 2/4, 3/4 and 4/4In 6/8, 9/8 and 12/8
Whole note4 beats2⅔ beats
Dotted half note3 beats2 beats
Half note2 beats1⅓ beats
Dotted quarter note1½ beats1 beat
Quarter note1 beat⅔ of a beat
Dotted eighth note¾ of a beat½ a beat
Eighth note½ a beat⅓ of a beat
Sixteenth note¼ of a beat⅙ of a beat

A dot adds to the note half of its value, and with a double dot the second dot adds half of what the first one adds. So a double-dotted quarter note lasts 1 + ½ + ¼ = 1¾ beats in 2/4, and ⅔ + ⅓ + ⅙ = 1⅙ beats in 6/8. Rests last exactly the same as their note, also with a dot.

In the exercise only notes that fit in the measure appear (a whole note does not fit in 2/4). And the whole rest appears only in 4/4: in a score it is used to show a whole measure of silence in any time signature, however long the measure lasts, so outside 4/4 it would not last the four beats of a whole note.

Frequently Asked Questions

How long does a dotted quarter note last?
It depends on the time signature. In 2/4, 3/4 and 4/4, where the beat is the quarter note, it lasts one and a half beats. In 6/8, 9/8 and 12/8, where the beat is the dotted quarter note, it lasts one beat.
How long does an eighth note last in 6/8?
One third of a beat. In 6/8 each beat is a dotted quarter note, which divides into three eighth notes.
How long does a dotted half note last?
Three beats in the meters with a 4 at the bottom, like 3/4, and two beats in the meters with an 8 at the bottom, like 6/8.
Why does the value of a note depend on the time signature?
Because what changes is the reference, not the note: its length is expressed in beats, and the note that lasts one beat is the quarter note in simple meters with a 4 at the bottom and the dotted quarter note in compound meters with an 8 at the bottom.
