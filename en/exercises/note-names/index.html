Note Names Quiz: Letters, Solfège and German

Test how well you know the names of the notes in the three most common systems: letters (C, D, E…, used in English-speaking countries), fixed-do solfège (Do, Re, Mi…, the names used in Spanish-, French- and Italian-speaking countries, where Do is always C) and the German system. There are 10 questions with instant answers, and the quiz includes the famous German trap: H and B.

Need to review first? Read the full guide to note names, with the equivalence tables for letters, fixed-do solfège and German.

More exercises: identify notes on the staff and write notes on the staff.

Frequently Asked Questions

What does this quiz practice?
The equivalences between note names as letters (C, D, E…), as fixed-do solfège (Do, Re, Mi…) and in German (where B natural is H and B♭ is B). Each question shows the answer and a short explanation.
How can I memorize the equivalences quickly?
With repetition: retake the quiz until you answer without hesitating. Start with the letters (C-D-E-F-G-A-B = Do-Re-Mi-Fa-Sol-La-Si) and leave German for last, because it only changes B natural (H) and B♭ (B).
