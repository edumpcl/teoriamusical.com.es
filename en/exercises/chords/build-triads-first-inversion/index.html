Build Triads in First Inversion

The third of the chord (the bass note in first inversion), the type of chord and the root are shown. Your task is to place the fifth and the root on the staff, in the correct octave. Click on the staff to place each note, and use the accidental buttons before placing it.

Do you want it as a game? In Billiards every ball carries a chord: you build its notes on the staff and, if they are right, the ball goes in, also in inversions. You can play alone or in class, taking turns.

What is first inversion?

In first inversion the third of the chord is in the bass. The other two notes (the fifth and the root) go above it in close position.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do you build a triad in first inversion?
In first inversion the third of the chord is in the bass. Write the third of the chord as the bass note. Then add the fifth above it (a third above the bass) and the root in the highest voice (a sixth above the bass).
Which intervals make up a first inversion, counted from the bass?
From the bass (the third of the chord) there is a third up to the fifth of the chord and a sixth up to the root. The exact qualities depend on the type: a major chord in first inversion has a minor 3rd and a minor 6th above the bass (E–G–C), while a minor chord has a major 3rd and a major 6th (C–E–A).
Why is it harder to build chords in inversion?
Because you have to think about which note is the root of the chord (it is not in the bass) and then work out the other notes from that root. You need to know the structure of each triad well before you can place it in an inversion.
What is the most common mistake when building a first inversion?
Putting the root in the bass instead of the third. You can catch the error by checking that the bass is really the third of the chord asked for. If the task is C major in first inversion, the bass must be E, not C.
How can I practice building chords in inversion?
First work out which note must go in the bass for the inversion asked. Then build the whole chord from the notes you know belong to it, and check that the intervals above the bass are right for that inversion.
