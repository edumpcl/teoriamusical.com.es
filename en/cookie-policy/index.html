2. What are cookies?

A cookie is a small file that the server of this Website sends to the browser of the device you connect from (computer, smartphone, tablet, etc.) in order to store information about your browsing from that device, which can later be retrieved.

The cookie guide published by the Spanish Data Protection Agency (AEPD) is available here (in Spanish):

http://www.agpd.es/portalwebAGPD/canaldocumentacion/publicaciones/common/Guias/Guia_Cookies.pdf

2.1. What types of cookies are there?

There are several categories of cookies, and one cookie can belong to more than one category:

2.1.1. Types of cookies by the entity that manages them

Depending on who manages the device or domain the cookies are sent from, and who processes the data obtained, we can distinguish:

• First-party cookies: Cookies sent to the user’s device from a device or domain managed by the owner or publisher of the Website, and from which the service requested by the user is provided.

• Third-party cookies: Cookies sent to the user’s device from a device or domain that is not managed by the owner or publisher of the Website, but by another entity that processes the data obtained through the cookies.

If cookies are installed from a device or domain managed by the owner or publisher of the Website itself, but the information they collect is managed by a third party, they cannot be considered first-party cookies.

2.1.2. Types of cookies by how long they stay active

• Session cookies: Cookies designed to collect and store data while the user visits a web page. They are usually used to store information that only needs to be kept to provide the service the user requested on one occasion.

• Persistent cookies: Cookies whose data stays stored on the device and can be accessed and processed for a period set by the party responsible for the cookie, which can range from a few minutes to several years.

2.1.3. Types of cookies by purpose

Depending on the purpose for which the data obtained through cookies is processed, we can distinguish:

• Technical cookies: Cookies that let the user browse a web page, platform or application and use the different options or services on it, for example controlling traffic and data communication, identifying the session, accessing restricted areas, remembering the items in an order, completing a purchase, registering for or taking part in an event, using security features while browsing, storing content for streaming video or sound, or sharing content on social networks.

• Personalization cookies: Cookies that let the user access the service with some predefined general characteristics based on criteria in the user’s device, such as the language, the type of browser used to access the service, the regional setting from which the service is accessed, etc.

• Analytics cookies: Cookies that let the party responsible for them track and analyze the behavior of the users of the websites they are linked to. The information collected through these cookies is used to measure the activity of the websites, applications or platforms and to build browsing profiles of their users, in order to make improvements based on the analysis of how users use the service.

• Advertising cookies: Cookies that let the publisher manage, as efficiently as possible, the ad spaces it may have included in a web page, application or platform from which it provides the requested service, based on criteria such as the content published or how often the ads are shown.

• Behavioral advertising cookies: Cookies that let the publisher manage, as efficiently as possible, the ad spaces it may have included in a web page, application or platform from which it provides the requested service. These cookies store information about user behavior obtained through continued observation of their browsing habits, which makes it possible to build a specific profile in order to show advertising based on it.

Edge: https://support.microsoft.com/en-us/microsoft-edge/delete-cookies-in-microsoft-edge

Firefox: https://support.mozilla.org/en-US/kb/enable-and-disable-cookies-website-preferences

In some cases it is necessary to install cookies so that the browser does not forget the user’s decision not to accept them.
