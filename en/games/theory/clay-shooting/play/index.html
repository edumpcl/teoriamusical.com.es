        Leaderboard

          Your nickname and your targets are published. Use a nickname, not your full name.

          Every target is a scale. First you write its key signature (that loads the shotgun) and then the scale, note by note (that is the shot). If both are right, you break the target: 1 point. If the key signature is wrong, the target flies and you can’t shoot; if any note of the scale is wrong, the shot misses. 3 targets per game. With several players the targets alternate and, if there is a tie, it is settled target by target. The local leaderboard is saved on this device; only the games you choose to add go to the world leaderboard: your nickname, your musician and your targets (more information).
