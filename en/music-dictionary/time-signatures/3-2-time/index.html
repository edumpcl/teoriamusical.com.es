3/2 Time Signature

3/2 time is a simple meter with three beats in which the note value that gets one beat is the half note (UK: minim). It is like a 3/4 "written large": three beats, but with the half note as the unit instead of the quarter note. It is common in early music.

What does 3/2 mean?

In the time signature, the top number 3 says there are three beats and the bottom number 2 says each beat is a half note (half of a whole note). Each half note divides into two quarter notes (UK: crotchets), so 3/2 is a simple meter with the accent pattern Strong–Weak–Weak.

How to count 3/2

Count "1 – 2 – 3", leaning on the 1. Each beat (half note) divides into two quarter notes, which gives six quarter notes per measure grouped 2+2+2:

Where is 3/2 used?

  • Renaissance music: 3/2 is one of the characteristic meters of Renaissance vocal polyphony.
  • Baroque music: common in sarabandes and in slow movements of a solemn character.

3/2 compared with 3/4

3/2 and 3/4 have the same three beats and the same accent pattern (strong–weak–weak). Only the note value that gets one beat changes: the half note in 3/2 and the quarter note in 3/4. Writing in 3/2 gives a broader, more spacious look on the page, typical of early music.

Do not confuse 3/2 with 6/4: both hold six quarter notes, but 3/2 groups them 2+2+2 (three simple beats) and 6/4 groups them 3+3 (two compound beats). Moving briefly from one grouping to the other is called a hemiola, a device common in Baroque music and in Spanish folk music.

How to practice 3/2

Practice the three beats with a steady pulse on the online metronome, and check the time signature of a score with the time signature analyzer.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is 3/2 time?
3/2 is a simple meter with three beats per measure, where each beat is a half note. Each half note divides into two quarter notes, giving six quarter notes per measure. It is common in Renaissance and Baroque music.
Is 3/2 simple or compound?
3/2 is a simple meter, because each beat (a half note) divides into two quarter notes.
How many beats are in a measure of 3/2?
Three beats, and the note value that gets one beat is the half note, with the accent pattern Strong–Weak–Weak.
What is the difference between 3/2 and 3/4?
They have the same three beats and the same accent pattern. Only the note value that gets one beat changes: the half note in 3/2 and the quarter note in 3/4.
