Major Seventh Chord

The major seventh chord (symbol Cmaj7) is a four-note chord: a major triad with a major 7th added on top. Its sound is bright, sweet and "floating", very typical of jazz, bossa nova and pop. It is one of the five seventh chords.

How to build a major seventh chord

Stack a major triad (major 3rd + perfect 5th) on the root and, above it, a major 7th. With C as the root:

NoteChord memberInterval above the root
CRoot—
EThirdMajor 3rd
GFifthPerfect 5th
BSeventhMajor 7th

The key to its color is the major 7th (C–B), only a half step (UK: semitone) below the octave. This sets it apart from the dominant seventh chord, which has a minor 7th (B♭) and sounds tense. The major seventh, in contrast, is stable and bright.

Major seventh chord symbols

ChordSymbol

On which degrees does it appear?

In the major scale, the major seventh chord is built on degrees I and IV. In C major they are Cmaj7 (I) and Fmaj7 (IV).

A note on terminology: "major seventh chord" is the name used with lead-sheet chord symbols and in jazz. In classical harmonic analysis this chord is simply the seventh chord built on degree I or IV, with no functional name of its own (unlike the dominant seventh or the leading-tone seventh).

Frequently Asked Questions

What is a major seventh chord?
It is a four-note chord made of a major triad plus a major 7th. On C the notes are C–E–G–B. It is written Cmaj7 and has a stable, bright, sweet sound, widely used in jazz and pop.
How do you build a major seventh chord?
Stack a major 3rd, a perfect 5th and a major 7th on the root. On C: C (root), E (major 3rd), G (perfect 5th) and B (major 7th).
What is the difference between a major seventh and a dominant seventh?
Only the seventh changes. The major seventh has a major 7th (B on C) and sounds stable and bright; the dominant seventh has a minor 7th (B♭) that creates tension and asks to resolve. The major triad is the same in both.
How is a major seventh chord written as a symbol?
With the root letter and "maj7", "M7" or the symbol "△7": Cmaj7 for C, Gmaj7 for G, and so on.
